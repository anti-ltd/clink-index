Text FX. iCloud Sync is free for everyone, and you can move the suggestion bar, the space bar row and the number row.

## Improved

- **Text FX.** Style the word you're typing from the keyboard. Bold, italic, underline, strike, plus Big, Small, Shake, and the rest, as text that works in any app.
- **iCloud Sync is free for everyone.** Syncing profiles, themes, clipboard text and vocabulary across your devices no longer needs a membership — the switch on the iCloud Sync page works for every user, and sync stays on even if a membership lapses.
- **Suggestion bar moves.** On Layout → Arrange, the suggestions and tools strip has a grip like the other rows. Drag it anywhere, even under a recent-emoji row. Tools, language, and in-bar panels stay in that strip.
- **Space bar row moves.** On Layout → Arrange, the bottom row has the same trash and grip as the letter rows. Drag it anywhere. Delete it if you want it gone.
- **Number row too.** With the digit strip on, it gets the same handles. Drag it between letter rows, or trash it to turn it off.
- **Long-press lives on Layout.** Accents, symbols, and numbers-on-hold are a folded section on the Layout page, next to Quick presets, instead of their own screen.
- **Presets come off as easily as they go on.** If a quick preset or long-press bundle is already on your layout, tap the minus to remove it.
- **Layout extras on Arrange too.** Quick presets, long-press, number row, split, and one-handed sit on Layout → Arrange as well, so you can reach them while moving keys.
- **Extra settings look like the rest.** Split, one-handed, and the other leftover cards no longer use a dashed outline. They're ordinary folded cards. Tap one to open it.
- **Number row size is a live preview.** Pick a preset, or drag Height and Text. Digits sit on a ghost letter row so you see the proportion, not just a number.
- **Clipboard chips look like chips.** Recent copies sit in little tiles, and a copied link shows the site (github.com) instead of http...
