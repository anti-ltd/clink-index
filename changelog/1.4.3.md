Lithuanian and Dutch, Layout Elements, custom keys you can paste in, a new app icon, and a tidier settings app.

## New

- **Layout Elements.** On Layout → Arrange, Element drops a solid bar onto your custom layout (between any keys, beside space, wherever keys go). Kinds: recent emoji, punctuation, clipboard chips (needs Full Access), numbers, a blank cursor pad (drag like the space trackpad, sideways or any direction), and tool shortcuts that look like a mini tools grid (same colours and corner rounding as the picker). Key background is optional, so strips and tool tiles can float. Pick how many slots (where it applies), tap a spot to use it, empty ones stay blank. Recent emoji and clipboard chips freeze while you type and refresh when the keyboard opens.
- **Paste as a custom key.** Layout editor can make a key that pastes whatever text is on the clipboard. Needs Full Access, same as the clipboard tools.
- **Clink Manager.** A Mac app under `manager/` runs make targets, shows live build output, and handles git (stage, commit, push, pull). Launch with `cd manager && make run`. Copy the folder to other projects and edit `config.json`.
- **Lithuanian.** Full dictionary, prediction, and autocorrect, with the Lithuanian QWERTY layout and all nine special letters (ą, č, ę, ė, į, š, ų, ū, ž) on long-press. Add it under Languages.
- **Dutch.** Full dictionary, prediction, and autocorrect on the standard QWERTY layout. Add it under Languages.
- **Sparkles mark languages with a neural model.** Add language shows ✨ beside every language that ships Clink's on-device neural engine, so you can tell at a glance without opening settings. Your active languages show the same mark.

## Improved

- **Tools only on Home.** The switch to collapse the app to just dictation and the tools now sits on the Home tab too, so you don't have to open General to find it.
- **New app icon.** A key with the sound ringing off it. Light, dark, and Clear each get their own.
- **Learned and custom words ask before deleting.** Removing a word or clearing the learned list now shows a confirmation, same idea as clearing restart history, so a stray tap while scrolling doesn't wipe anything.
- **Keys beside space is gone.** The separate Layout row and always-on flank store are removed. Put keys beside the space bar in Layout → Arrange, or use Quick presets. Always-on keys you had move into each custom layout on update.
- **Row layout moved to Layout.** Number row, split keyboard, and one-handed mode now sit on the Layout page below Quick presets, with row insets draggable on the preview. Keys drops its Rows tab and keeps Shape and Touch.
- **Quick presets back on Layout.** Customize → Layout shows an expandable Quick presets section again for one-tap keys beside the space bar.
- **Layout preview drops Try it out.** The typing field is gone from the Layout page; the keyboard preview stays live for visual feedback.
- **Tools Access chips have labels.** The two switches under the Access preview (Tools button, Icons before typing) show their names beside the icons, so you can tell them apart without guessing.
- **Snappier default key feel.** Clink now ships with instant highlight and no press linger or min highlight, like the stock keyboard. The old soft, springy feel lives on as the **Liquid** preset under Animation → Feel.
- **Arrange keys floats free.** Layout → Arrange keys drops the collapsible **Arrange** header; Key and Row, the how-to line, and create-from-preset sit in one open card below the preview.
- **Haptics feel pad floats free.** The feel control sits on the page like Sound's volume waveform, without a collapsible section or extra card around it.
- **Clipboard History swipes like the keyboard panel.** On History, swipe left on a clip for copy, pin, or delete; swipe right to assign it to a group. Same actions you get in the keyboard panel, without switching apps.
- **Tools mode trims Integrations for free users.** Apple Intelligence no longer shows Assists or Translation, and the Cling tab is gone. Dictation and AI Tools stay.
- **Tools menu cards stay flat on glass themes.** Grid and Cards picker rows used to go translucent on glass keyboards. They now use the same solid fill as on flat themes, so the tint and labels stay readable.
- **iCloud Sync on the hero.** Extras → iCloud Sync puts **Enable** on the demo card; what-travels rows float below without a section header. Clipboard and learned-words privacy hints removed.
- **Tool heroes say Enable.** Every Tools page labels the hero switch **Enable** at the top instead of repeating the page title.
- **Heatmap overlay on the Overlays hero.** Extras → Overlays → Telemetry puts the heatmap switch on the demo as **Enable**; the samples readout floats on its own card below.
- **Key deformation on the Experimental hero.** Extras → Experimental → Deform puts form layout on the demo as **Enable**; **Control** below holds free corners only.
- **Tap correction on the Experimental hero.** Extras → Experimental → Correct puts correct-my-taps on the demo as **Enable**; the card below is **Control** (strength and overlay field).
- **Landing bias on the Experimental hero.** Extras → Experimental → Learn puts the learn-my-bias switch on the demo card as **Enabled**; the Landing bias card is gone.
- **Dictation assist on by default.** Clean up what I say and Run tools by voice both start on for new setups. Turn them off under Dictation or Integrations if you want raw transcripts only.
- **Swipe down for accent on by default.** Gestures → Accents turns the quick downward flick on for new setups. Turn it off there if you prefer hold-only accents.
- **Cursor movement on the Gestures hero.** Swipe the demo on Customize → Gestures → Cursor to switch Spacebar, Trackpad, and Combined, same as picking a dictation style. The chip row below the hero is gone; Feel is what's left on the tab.
- **Languages page tidied up.** Typing stays on Customize → Languages but starts folded, along with Switching language. Your own languages and imported dictionaries are back on their own pages, open when you arrive.
- **Learning toggle sits on the neural hero.** Customize → Text → Learning matches Popups: turn learning on from the demo card; what's below is the neural engine and learned words.
- **Learned words has its own page.** The word list and language tabs live on Learned words, open when you arrive.
- **Custom words, text replacements, and imported dictionaries each have their own page.** Customize → Text → Words is three rows now; Create and the list on each page start expanded.
- **Popups Style and Animation start folded.** Customize → Popups opens on the demo and the master switch; expand Style or Animation when you want to tune them.
- **Tools button is just the grid icon.** The suggestion bar and any Tools key on your layout show the same grid mark, with no "Tools" label. The Tools settings preview matches.

## Fixed

- **Copied IP addresses show as IPs.** A clip like 10.0.0.1 was tagged as a link because it is also a valid web host.
- **Function-key paint falls through.** Shift and the other modifiers pick up the All function keys colour when they don't have a colour of their own.
- **Portuguese esta after a pronoun becomes está.** ela esta and você esta follow the verb reading. por esta stays the demonstrative.
- **stakking becomes stalling as you type.** The fast suggestion pass was picking stalking instead.
- **Half-typed words keep real completions on the bar.** Typing hel was offering he and her ahead of hello.
- **More of the app is translated.** New settings labels, empty states, and editor copy that were still English in other languages now match the rest of the app.
- **Tool back from layout shortcuts.** In grid tool access, the back chevron in a tool panel returns to the tools grid when you came from the grid, and to the keyboard when you opened that tool from a layout shortcut (Open panel key or tool-shortcuts tile).
- **Element strip press.** Tapping a tool tile (or any Element slot) no longer paints the whole bar blue. Only the tile you hit flashes.
- **Language cap styles are independent.** The suggestion-bar language button, and each layout language key, keep their own flag / globe / code setting. Editing one no longer changes the others.
- **Old space-bar keys survive an update.** Configs that stored comma and period beside space the old way keep those keys on the flanks after upgrading, instead of losing them or parking them on the wrong row.
- **Keys beside space land on the right side.** On a custom layout, adding a key left or right of space no longer parks it past the space bar or return. Existing misplaced ones move back when you relaunch.
- **Free tools keep the short keyboard height.** Opening dictation or AI tools on the locked keyboard no longer stretches it to full key height. The compact wall size stays put for the whole session.
- **Number-row flank keys resize on their own.** Shrinking the key left of the digits no longer widens the one on the right to 1.7 (and vice versa). Each side keeps its own width.
- **Swipe trail shows while you glide.** The finger trail had zero height beside the touch surface, and trail updates weren't always reaching the draw layer. Both fixed, the stroke tracks your finger again.
- **Custom key symbols stay the same in light and dark mode.** SF Symbols on painted keys no longer flip outline/fill when you switch appearance.
- **Slide from 123 on custom layouts.** Hold 123, slide to a symbol, and let go works again on layouts you built or edited. The movable 123 key now gets the same press-and-slide peek as the built-in one.
- **Custom and learned words fix typos now.** A name or brand you added (like SwiftKey) used to block autocorrect on the exact spelling but never helped when you mistyped it, because the dictionary trie had never seen it. Typos now route to your saved word, and typing it all lowercase restores the casing you saved.
- **Dictation follows your keyboard language.** Voice typing now listens in whichever language you're switched to on the keyboard, not your iPhone's default. Add Italian under Languages, switch to it on the keyboard, and dictation transcribes in Italian.
- **Clipboard panel scroll and swipe work in the keyboard.** Saved clips no longer stretch to the full panel height, so the list can scroll. Row swipes (copy, pin, delete, group) use UIKit pan arbitration instead of SwiftUI drag, which fights the scroll view in the extension, and the scroll surface has a touch backing so the first drag is not dropped.
