## New

- **Key shapes.** Design ▸ Style Control has a Key shape row: Rectangle, Capsule, Circle, Diamond, Hexagon, Flat hexagon and Pentagon, on any material — a hexagon under 3D ▸ Mechanical is a moulded hexagonal cap. Every shape keeps your key width, key height, corner radius and both spacings, and a new Merge rows switch beside Join rows (off by default) lets the rows tuck into each other. On a merged hexagon board every key is cut from one cell lattice, so a letter is one cell, the space bar a strip of cells, and the space row tucks in exactly like the letter rows — the pitch is even down the whole board. Rows tuck into each other a row climbs into the grooves of the row above exactly as far as the two outlines allow, keeping the larger of your row and key spacing between them, and the height that frees goes back to the keys, so the keyboard stays the height you set. A wide key stretches through a flat crown rather than flattening its points, so the space bar is a hexagonal bar — and, having no groove, keeps the plain gap. Theme files and plugin key styles can ask for the shapes by name (`hex`, `hexFlat`, `circle`, `capsule`, `diamond`, `pentagon`).
- **Honeycomb layout.** A real hexagonal board: QWERTY's letters in their usual order over four staggered rows of whole cells (w e t y i o · q s r g u k p · ⇧ a d f h j l ⌫ · z x c v b n m), and a bottom row of cells too — 123, comma, a two-cell space, period, return. Every row sits half a cell across from the one above, which is what lets shaped keys sit in each other's grooves all the way down.
- **Honeycomb theme (debug builds).** Honeycomb and Honeycomb Dark, hexagonal cells on a lilac-grey or charcoal deck, on the Debug shelf while they are tuned.
- **Keys that show on every layout, again.** Tap a key beside the space bar in Layout → Arrange and turn on **Show on every layout** under Geometry: it moves out of that layout and sits beside the space bar on all of them, presets included, so a language, emoji or panel key is set up once. It keeps its side of the space bar, can still be edited, resized and reordered from any custom layout, and turning the switch off (or dragging it up into a letter row) hands it back to the layout you're on. Shift, 123, space, return and delete stay per layout. Keys that went always-on before this was taken out were already copied into each of your custom layouts, and stay there.

## Improved

- **A tidier one-handed gutter.** The side arrow and the expand button are one control now: swipe it toward the other side to move the keys there, tap it to go back to the full keyboard. The extra-keys button moves up beside it in the toolbar row, so numbers, emoji or your own keys fill the gutter's whole height.
