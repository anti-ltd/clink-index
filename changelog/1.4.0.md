Five new languages, a markedly more accurate autocorrect, and a rebuilt swipe-down accent gesture you can see and cancel.

## New

- **Greek, Vietnamese, and Indonesian.** All three arrive with full dictionaries and on-device neural prediction. Greek gets its own layout; Vietnamese types over QWERTY with the existing Telex, VNI, and VIQR input modes.
- **Brazilian Portuguese and Mexican Spanish are keyboards in their own right.** They used to borrow European Portuguese and Spanish dictionaries; now each has its own, so regional spelling and everyday slang — ahorita, órale, jaja, porfa — are words Clink knows rather than words it corrects.
- **Arabic and Bulgarian prediction.** Both languages now get full prediction and autocorrect.
- **Swipe down for an accent is now a live pull.** The accent slides in over the key as you drag, so you can see what you're about to get. Release to commit it, or drag back up to change your mind — it no longer fires the instant you move.
- **Undo and redo while designing.** The theme builder and the layout editor both keep a history, so an experiment that goes wrong is one tap back.
- **Built-in keys can really be removed.** Deleting shift, backspace, 123, space, or return from a custom layout used to look like it worked and then spring back. Now it sticks.
- **Numbers on the top letter row.** A new "Number row long-press" preset under Keys puts each top-row key's digit a swipe or hold away (q gives 1, w gives 2, on to p for 0). It reads positions, not letters, so it fits AZERTY, Dvorak, and any custom layout, not just QWERTY.
- **Swipe down for a quick accent.** Flick straight down on a key to drop in its first accent instantly — swipe down on "e" for "è" — instead of holding for the popup. Turn it on under Keys; it needs accent popups enabled.

## Improved

- **Autocorrect is markedly more accurate.** Badly mistyped words get fixed instead of shrugged at ("rovqnda" → "provando"), missing accents are restored ("perche" → "perché", "tambien" → "también", "aqui" → "aquí"), genuinely missed spaces split ("vabene" → "va bene"), and Clink stops swapping words you clearly typed for rarer lookalikes ("faster" is no longer "corrected" to "carter"). Real words are also no longer sliced into fragments. Measured across a new English, Italian, and Spanish test corpus, silent fixes went from 67% to 97% recall at 95% precision, with no over-corrections left.
- **Italian prediction rebuilt.** Its neural model is roughly twice the size and noticeably better at reading the whole sentence.
- **A labeled Tools button, and a roomier tools menu.** The button now reads "Tools" while you haven't typed anything, and the menu itself is wider, scrolls, and explains what each entry does.
- **Apple Intelligence lives under Integrations.** It moved off the Engine page into its own card with a plain-English status line, so the Engine page is now just Learning, Words, and Neural.
- **A new Keyboard tools page.** Calculator, Clipboard, Emoji, Notepad, Translate, and AI Tools are a card grid with descriptions and on/off badges, plus a shortcut to rearrange them.
- **The Arabic keyboard drops its shift key.** Arabic is caseless, so the key did nothing; the bottom row's letters spread into the space it leaves behind.
- **The Feel page opens on Haptics** rather than Sound.
- **Localization refreshed across every language.** Swept the app for newly added interface strings and brought all eleven supported languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Malay, Hungarian, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean) back to full coverage, so nothing in Settings falls back to English.

## Fixed

- **The Neural stats screen reported the wrong vocabulary size.** It read the older word-level vocabulary while the engine was actually running the newer subword one, so the number on screen didn't describe the model doing the work.
- **AI writing tools could crash.** Two requests arriving at once could take the keyboard down with them; generations are now serialized.
- **The first word of a sentence capitalizes even when you type fast.** Beating the keyboard to the next letter used to leave you with "ciao. sto…".
- **"i've" reliably becomes "I've".** An apostrophe that landed as a curly opening quote used to defeat the fix; now it corrects the quote too.
- **Flicks and swipe typing no longer fight each other.** A downward flick won't drop a stray digit or accent on top of a word you just glided.
- **The tools menu style setting applies everywhere.** The bar icon and the 123 slide-up could disagree about which style you'd picked.
- **The splash screen says it's skippable.** Tap and hold to skip.
- **AI writing tools recover after a cancelled request.** Stopping or interrupting a generation could wedge the AI tools so the next request failed. Clink now resets the session behind the scenes and the next request works.
- **Text replacements follow your sentence case.** A lowercase replacement now capitalizes its first letter when it expands at the start of a sentence ("omw" → "On my way") while staying lowercase mid-sentence, and it still honours shift and caps-lock — matching how the rest of your typing is cased.

## Removed

- **Per-key swipe-down faces.** Swiping down on a key is the quick-accent gesture now, so a custom key can no longer bind its own down action — two bindings on one gesture meant neither felt reliable. Swipe left and swipe right are unchanged. If you'd set a down face on a custom key, it will stop firing; re-apply the "Number row long-press" preset under Keys to get your digits back on the top row.
