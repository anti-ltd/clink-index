Vietnamese Telex input.

## New

- **Vietnamese Telex input.** While Vietnamese is your sole active language, modifier
  keys compose in place: `i`+`r`→`ỉ`, `aa`→`â`, `dd`→`đ`, and the other standard
  Telex tones. Bilingual combined mode keeps typing keys literally so English isn't
  affected. Toggle under Localization → Input. (`TelexCompositor`, `AccentMap`)
- **Swipe between settings tabs.** Swipe left or right on any tabbed settings page
  (Emoji, Keys, Feel, Text, Gestures, and the rest) to step tabs without reaching
  for the picker. Clamps at the ends, skips disabled tabs, plays nice with scroll.
  (`SwipeStepModifier`, `Binding.stepTab`)
- **First-run intro.** Five-card welcome on first launch: what Clink is, then a
  setup card that sends you to Permissions. Theme-aware pill and backdrop. Replay
  from the hand-wave button in the home footer. (`OnboardingView`, `AppModel.showIntro`)
- **Symbol long-press preset.** New Layout → Quick preset that adds a symbol to
  each letter's long-press bar (A → @, S → #, D → $, and so on) without touching
  accents, taps, or shift. Per layout, like the other presets. Corner hints show
  the symbol when applied. (`SymbolMap`, `KeyboardLayout.symbolLongPress`)
- **Swipe accuracy overhaul.** Big glide-decoding pass after "the swipe typing is
  trash" feedback. Wrong guesses stay on the suggestion bar for one-tap recovery;
  backspace drops the whole swiped word and brings alternates back. No-match glides
  restore the letter you started on. Contractions swipe now (`don't`, `i'm`, `c'est`).
  Learned words and recency boost glide picks like the bar. (`SwipeDecoder`,
  `SuggestionEngine.swipeCandidates`, `pendingSwipe`)
- **Double-letter glide hint (experimental).** A dwell or loop on a key nudges
  double-letter words (`too` over `to`). Mild on purpose; geometry still wins. Needs
  on-device tuning. (`SwipeDecoder.dwellKeys`, `doubleDwellBonus`)
- **Swipe trail styles.** Seven trail looks under Gestures → Swipe → Trail & ripple:
  Solid, Neon, Rainbow, Sparkle, Comet, Fire, and Ocean. Thickness slider goes to
  12 pt. Cosmetic only; the touch surface redraws the bitmap. (`SwipeTrailStyle`,
  `NativeSwipeTrailView`)
- **Trail the finger.** Toggle keeps only the tail of the glide lit; the streak
  fades as you move. Tail length slider (40–300 pt). Works with every style.
  (`swipeTrailTrim`, `swipeTrailMaxLength`)
- **Tapered trail width.** Start width and end width ramp the stroke along the
  path. 1.0× on both is the old uniform look. (`swipeTrailStartWidth`,
  `swipeTrailEndWidth`)
- **Personal mistype correction.** Learns the fixes you make over and over (`thw`
  → `the`), even when the dictionary never flags them. Tapping a bar replacement
  or keeping an autocorrect teaches it; reverting fades the bad fix. Per layout
  and language, 21-day decay. Confident fixes commit silently on space. Gated by
  the learning switch; off in secure fields. (`MistypeLearner`,
  `KeyboardViewController.applyPendingAutocorrect`)
- **Real-word typo correction.** Catches valid-but-wrong words one edit from a
  common neighbour (`tat` → `that`, `kust` → `just`). Silent on space only when
  context backs it; otherwise waits in the bar. Common words never get rewritten
  on frequency alone. (`Lexicon.editNeighbors`, `SuggestionEngine.realWordCorrection`)
- **Run-on word splitting.** Repairs fused words (`thatwas` → `that was`,
  `helloworld` → `hello world`). One half may carry a typo fix; contractions like
  `dont` stay on the punctuation path. (`SuggestionEngine.runOnSplit`)
- **Context-aware suggestion bar.** Ranks by what follows the word you just typed,
  not raw frequency. After *happy*, *birthday* beats *big*; after *merry*, *christmas*
  surfaces even when rare. O(1) lookup per candidate; autocorrect unchanged.
  (`SuggestionEngine.rankScore`, `MergedLexicon.followerLogProbabilities`)
- **Recency-aware completions.** Words you just typed get a small boost in the bar
  so names and one-offs resurface immediately. Memory only, per message, capped below
  corpus frequency. (`UserAdaptation.recencyBoost`, `SuggestionEngine.rankScore`)
- **Split keyboard.** Layout & Keys → Split keyboard opens a gap down the middle;
  keys pull to each edge for thumb reach. Gap width slider and per-row split
  steppers. Optional adaptive letter sides and sliding space bar. Letters plane
  only; off by default. (`KeyboardSettings.splitLetters`, `KeyboardCanvas.injectSplit`)
- **Contact-name suggestions toggle.** Text → Suggestions → Contact names controls
  whether Contacts names appear in the bar. Off by default. Text replacements from
  Settings are always honored. (`KeyboardSettings.contactSuggestionsEnabled`)
- **Number / phone keypads.** Number, decimal, and phone fields get a proper digit
  pad instead of the alpha keyboard. Inherits your theme; no suggestion bar.
  (`KeyboardController.NumericPad`, `KeyboardCanvas.padRows`)
- **Host field-trait parity.** Honors `autocapitalizationType`, secure fields (no
  suggestions, autocorrect, or learning), `autocorrectionType == .no`, and smart
  quote/dash off for code fields. (`KeyboardViewController.hostIsSecure`,
  `SmartPunctuation.edit`)
- **Separate vs Combined typing languages.** Localization → Typing mode: Combined
  runs all enabled languages at once (default); Separate runs one. Swipe up on Return
  to pick the active language. Optional per-language layout swap.
  (`KeyboardSettings.languageInputMode`, `LocalizationView`)
- **Send a clip to Cling.** Clipboard → Cling → Send to Cling: swipe a text card
  right for a Cling button that pins it as a note (`cling://create/note?…`). Text
  only; needs Full Access. (`KeyboardSettings.clipboardClingShare`, `shareToCling`)
- **Per-row glass merge.** Performance → Responsiveness → Rendering: merge Liquid
  Glass per row instead of the whole keyboard. Less GPU heat during fast typing;
  off by default. (`KeyboardSettings.glassPerRowMerge`, `PerformanceView`)
- **Backup & restore moved into Profiles.** Save, share, import, and reset now live
  at the bottom of Profiles instead of a standalone sheet. (`BackupControls` in
  `ProfilesView`)
- **Export a single profile.** Each profile's ⋯ menu can export just that setup as
  a `.clinkconfig` without your live settings. (`ProfilesView`, `ConfigDocument`)
- **Importing a config asks what to do.** Replace current settings or create a new
  profile from the file. (`AppModel.createProfileFromImportedConfig`)
- **Responsiveness card gained Custom + Edit.** Fine-tune spring speed, bloom, press
  linger, and compute delay from one popup. (`ResponsivenessFineTune`)
- **App language.** Container UI in ten languages from Localization → App language.
  Instant switch, no relaunch; stored outside profiles. Separate from keyboard input
  languages. (`LanguageManager`, `LocalizationView.appLanguageSection`)
- **Glass fallback badge.** When Liquid Glass degrades, a badge on the suggestion bar
  explains why (memory, thermal, Low Power, Reduce Motion, pre-iOS 26). Tap for
  detail. (`MotionProfile.glassDegradeReason`, `glassDebugBadge`)
- **Home settings search.** Search bar on the home screen finds pages and individual
  controls ("Roundness", "Auto-correction"). Tapping a result opens the right tab,
  scrolls, and flashes the control. `make check-search` fails if you add a control
  without indexing it. (`SettingsCatalog`, `ClinkContent.searchField`)
- **Image clipboard history.** Copied images save to clipboard history with a
  thumbnail. Tap to copy back (keyboards can't paste images into fields). Full
  resolution kept on disk; needs Full Access. (`ClipboardEntry`, `ClipboardManager`)
- **Translate panel.** In-keyboard translate without leaving the field. Apple
  Intelligence or offline Translation packs. (`TranslateView`, `TranslatePanel`)
- **Custom dictionary.** Suggestions → Custom: hand-add words the keyboard should
  always offer and never "correct". On-device App Group file. (`UserAdaptation`)
- **Simple / Advanced settings mode.** Home toggle hides fine-tune controls behind
  Advanced in the app and extension.
- **Keep effects on in Low Power Mode.** Theme → ⋯ toggle (on by default) drops glass
  and glow on battery saver. Thermal and memory fallbacks unchanged.
  (`reduceEffectsOnLowPower`, `MotionProfile.setRespectLowPower`)
- **In-app changelog.** Footer changelog button opens baked per-version cards from
  `CHANGELOG.md` (`make changelog`), no network.
- **Swipe / glide typing.** Trace across letter keys; the decoder scores by key
  proximity forward and reverse coverage, with frequency as a tie-break.
  (`SwipeDecoder`, `KeyTouchRouter`)
- **Next-word prediction & completion.** Ranked from bundled n-gram lexicons
  (en/fr/es/de/it/pt/ru via `make lexicons`). Memory-mapped `.clex` files.
  (`Lexicon`, `Tools/GenerateLexicons.swift`)
- **On-device learning.** Opt-in store remembers words you commit, boosts bar
  picks you keep, suppresses fixes you reject. One App Group JSON file.
  (`UserAdaptation`)
- **Apple Intelligence backend.** Optional on-device AI (iOS 26+, A17 Pro / M-series)
  for bar completions, AI autocorrect, and AI translate. Off by default; never blocks
  the offline bar. (`AIEngine`)
- **Sparkle marker on AI suggestions.** AI completions show a ✨ glyph and accent
  tint in the bar. (`SuggestionBar`)
- **Custom keys.** Layout → Custom: text keys with long-press alternates or function
  keys (cursor, plane switch, emoji, backspace). (`CustomKey`, `CustomKeysView`)
- **Grid clipboard.** Clipboard panel can render as a grid with swipe actions.
  (`ClipboardPanel`, `SwipeRow`)
- **Motion engine.** Dedicated animation layer for key press and panels with tunable
  profiles. (`Motion`, `MotionProfile`, `MotionSequence`)
- **Key previews & key faces.** Per-key preview rendering and customizable faces in
  Keys settings.
- **Suggestion-bar font** setting, richer in-app keyboard preview, and suggestion-bar
  padding control.
- **Auto keyboard height.** Height adjusts for the default icon set; option to hide
  the suggestion bar.
- New app icon.
- **Multi-language typing.** Enable multiple languages in Localization; completions,
  predictions, and swipe vocab merge across them. A word only autocorrects when it's
  wrong in all active languages. (`KeyboardSettings.keyboardLanguages`)
- **Revert auto-correct on delete.** Delete right after autocorrect restores what you
  typed and leaves that word alone on the next space. On by default.
- **German (QWERTZ) layout.** ü on the top row, ö ä on the home row. Auto-paired when
  you add German. (`KeyboardLayout`)
- **Custom keyboard layouts.** Layout → Custom layouts: start from a preset or blank,
  edit keys per row, save to the picker. (`KeyboardLayout.customLayouts`)
- **Drag-to-arrange custom keys.** Place custom keys at any slot; drag in the Keys
  preview. (`PlacedCustomKey`, `CustomKeyArrangeOverlay`)
- **Interactive spacing on the Keys page.** Drag handles on the preview for bar
  padding, keyboard padding, and per-row inset. Double-tap to reset.
  (`PaddingDragHandle`, `RowInsetHandles`)
- **Always-show-capitals key faces.** Keys → Faces draws uppercase labels while typing
  still respects shift.
- **Autocorrect in search fields.** Text → Suggestions toggle to autocorrect in search
  bars that normally turn it off. Never in URL, email, number, or phone fields. Off
  by default. (`KeyboardSettings.autocorrectEverywhere`)
- **Clink Flow layout.** Built-in two-thumb letter layout in the Layout picker, not a
  QWERTY remap. (`KeyboardLayout` id `clinkflow`)
- **Pistachio theme.** New solid preset: pistachio background, cream keys, olive
  accent. (`Theme+Pistachio`)
- **Emoji key in the bottom row.** Emoji settings → Emoji key places a dedicated 🙂
  beside 123. Off by default. (`KeyboardSettings.emojiKeyInRow`)
- **Keyboard entrance animation.** Fade, Rise, Scale, or Drop on appear; None is
  default. (`KeyboardSettings.keyboardEntrance`, `KeyboardEntrance`)
- **Emoji grid density.** Sliders for glyph scale and cell spacing in Emoji settings.
  (`emojiGlyphScale`, `emojiCellSpacing`)
- **Auto-capture clipboard on open.** Pull the pasteboard into history when the
  keyboard appears (needs Full Access). Off by default.
  (`KeyboardSettings.autoCopyOnKeyboardOpen`)
- **Inline-icons bar style.** Spread panel icons across the suggestion bar instead of
  text labels or the cycling picker. (`PanelPickerStyle.inlineIcons`)

## Improved

- CI and build pipeline fixes
- Discord notification integration
- **Accent picker: slide sideways, no reaching up.** Hold a letter to raise the
  diacritic bar, then pick a variant by sliding left or right at the same height.
  No more lifting up into the bar. A plain hold-and-release still keeps the base
  letter. Once you start sliding, the highlight grabs the swatch under your finger
  and tracks the whole bar, so it won't blank if you slide back past where you
  started, even with the bar shoved against a screen edge. Drag down off the key to
  cancel back to the plain letter. (`TouchEngine+Accent.swift`, `accentEngaged`)
- **Per-build changelog and What's New.** Every build gets its own changelog section
  tagged `(B{n})`. `make bump` ships `[Unreleased]`, writes the What's New intro
  from your headlines, and bakes the in-app copy. Upgraders see the sheet on first
  launch, or replay it from the sparkles button in the home footer.
  (`BumpRelease.swift`, `ChangelogParser`, `WhatsNewView`)
- **Long-press bars now combine instead of overriding.** Accents, symbol preset, and
  custom alternates share one bar. Base glyph first, accents, symbol last.
  (`KeyboardCanvas+KeySpecs`, `AccentMap`/`SymbolMap`)
- **Long-press works while swipe typing is on.** A still hold raises the bar; a real
  glide cancels the pending hold first. (`TouchEngine+Lifecycle`)
- **Emoji picker scrolls horizontally by default.** `emojiScrollDirection` default is
  now horizontal. Still switchable in settings.
- **Swipe across the emoji grid to switch tabs.** Swipe the non-scrolling axis to page
  categories; tracks your finger with elastic offset. Toggle in Emoji → Scroll
  (`emojiCrossAxisSwitchesTab`, default on).
- **Faster suggestion bar and autocorrect.** Bar paints synchronously from the local
  text mirror instead of waiting on debounce + cross-process reads. Space-commit
  skips the checker for confidently common words. (`scheduleSuggestionUpdate`,
  `SuggestionEngine.resolveCorrection`)
- **Lighter adaptive-hitbox prediction.** One lexicon walk per keystroke instead of
  two when adaptive hitboxes are on. (`Lexicon.nextLetterWeights`)
- **Key magnifier snaps during fast typing.** Popup emerge spring skipped mid-burst on
  older GPUs. (`BalloonPopup`, `MotionProfile.prefersInstantKeyPress`)
- **Autocorrect no longer overwrites real words.** Silent fix on space must beat the
  word you actually typed, not just clear a confidence bar. Regional spellings stay;
  "yeah" → "yea" and "gonna" → "gone" are rejected. (`CorrectionScorer.verdict`)
- **Lighter suggestion hot path.** One text-proxy read per debounce tick, Schwartzian
  sort for bar ranking, precomputed probability table for hitboxes, AI context capped
  at ~200 chars. (`KeyboardViewController.debouncedSuggestionTick`, `AIEngine.completions`)
- **Snappier fast typing, less heat.** Press-glow blur dropped during bursts; burst
  window widened; learned-word saves moved off the typing thread. (`KeyView.pressGlowLayer`,
  `UserAdaptation`)
- **Faster keyboard launch.** Supplementary lexicon (Contacts + text replacements)
  loads after appear instead of blocking first keystroke. (`KeyboardViewController.viewDidAppear`)
- **Smoother emoji scrolling.** Cell frames captured in scroll-stable space; screen
  mapping only when a gesture needs it. (`EmojiCanvas`)
- **Custom key editor honors theme roundness.** Glyph keys, chips, and fields match
  key roundness. (`CustomKeyEditorBody`, `TextFieldRow`)
- **Key size popover no longer duplicates Roundness and Spacing.** Size holds only
  geometry unique to it. (`KeySizeFineTune`)
- **Reworded the in-app hints and tooltips.** Plainer voice across settings subtitles
  and help text.
- **"Reduce effects under load" renamed and defaults off.** Now covers thermal too,
  not just Low Power Mode. Memory pressure always sheds glass (or iOS kills the
  keyboard). Note in Theme → ⋯. (`reduceEffectsOnLowPower`, `MotionProfile.refreshTier`)
- **Emoji set updated to Unicode 17.0.** Regenerated via `make emoji`. New glyphs need
  an OS with the Unicode 17.0 emoji font.
- Settings that don't apply to the current configuration are hidden instead of shown
  disabled, in the app and extension.
- **Languages seed from the device.** Fresh install picks keyboard languages from the
  phone's preferred list. Autocorrect runs across all active languages.
- **Keys page reorganised.** Size card is geometry only; home-row inset toggle gone
  (use per-row drag handles). Keys preview is non-interactive.
- **Settings pages consolidated.** Feel (sounds + haptics), Keys (hitboxes + layout),
  and Text (automation + adaptation + suggestions) merged fewer home cards.
  (`FeelView`, `KeysView`, `TextView`)

## Fixed

- App Store Connect submission fixes
- **No more function-key flash on plane switch.** Release tint on the key you tapped
  now snaps off in the same no-animation transaction as the plane flip, so glass
  re-merge can't bleed it onto steady keys. (`KeyboardCanvas.plane`, `clearAllPress`)
- **Recents row no longer reshuffles mid-tap.** Reorder deferred until 1.5s idle or
  next panel open. (`recordRecentEmoji` debounce)
- **Emoji preview no longer hijacks swipes.** Preview on Emoji settings is passive;
  category swipe stays keyboard-only.
- **Reliable taps on the suggestion bar and panels.** Chips and panel icons use UIKit
  tap capture like the keys, not SwiftUI `DragGesture`. (`KeyboardReliableButtonStyle`,
  `TapCaptureView`)
- **Swipe no longer jams from vowels.** Accent long-press doesn't arm on letter keys
  while swipe is on. (`TouchEngine.touchDown`)
- **Gutter taps no longer miss.** Touch surface carries a near-invisible fill so
  `.clear` doesn't drop hits between keys. Rejects taps above the bar.
  (`KeyGridTouchView.captureFill`)
- **Glide typing now reaches accented words.** Decoder folds diacritics onto base keys
  for scoring. (`SwipeDecoder` fold cache)
- **Glide no longer collapses a long zig-zag to a short prefix.** Length-shortfall
  penalty stops "teste" decoding as "te". (`SwipeDecoder.lengthShortfallWeight`)
- **Typed Portuguese accents auto-restore.** `voce` → `você`, `nao` → `não`, and so
  on, with a frequency gate so `e` vs `é` needs context. (`Lexicon.accentVariants`)
- **Applying a profile no longer resets newer settings.** Profile apply overlays onto
  current settings instead of replacing missing keys with defaults. Same for older
  `.clinkconfig` imports. (`ProfileConfig.apply`, `AppModel.importConfiguration`)
- **Taps in keyboard top/bottom padding now register.** Touch surface spans the full
  padded footprint. (`KeyboardCanvas` touch overlay)
- **Panel controls no longer need multiple taps.** PrimitiveButtonStyle with a
  zero-distance drag gesture for reliable hits in the input window.
  (`KeyboardReliableButtonStyle`)
- **Switching profiles no longer wipes custom layouts.** `customLayouts` excluded from
  profile snapshots; existing profiles heal on apply. (`ProfileConfig.layoutKeys`)
- **Liquid Glass no longer flattens after a minute of typing.** Memory listener now
  reacts to `.critical` only, not routine `.warning` in the extension sandbox.
  (`armMemoryPressureSource`)
- **Fast typing drops fewer keys.** Instant-press engages at ~5.5 keys/s; glyph layer
  snaps during bursts; glass shows flat tint overlay instead of re-rastering the lens
  each key. (`MotionProfile.burstGap`, `KeyView.glassPressOverlay`)
- **AI completions now match your typing language.** Completer takes active keyboard
  language(s) as an explicit directive. (`AIEngine.completions`)
- **Autocorrect no longer mangles capitalised words.** Transposition fix keeps the
  leading capital at the front. (`SuggestionEngine.transpositionFix`)
- **Smoother Liquid Glass key presses.** Bloom isolated to the pressed key (~6× fewer
  lens re-rasters per frame). Swipe ripple unchanged. (`KeyView`, `TouchEngine.recomputePressed`)
- **Smoother typing under memory pressure.** Extension watches memory pressure and
  falls back to solid keys until it clears. (`MotionProfile.setMemoryPressure`)
- **Dropped keystrokes when typing fast.** Touch router double-buffers key frames across
  layout swaps. (`TouchEngine.update`)
- **Function-key flash.** Plane-switch flash on function keys fixed, plus App Store
  compliance for ITMS-90737 / ITMS-90788.
- **Number row dead on the symbols/numbers planes.** Digit strip keyspecs built on
  every plane, not just letters. (`KeyboardCanvas.buildKeySpecs`)
- Settings schema stays backward-compatible across upgrades (`SettingsBackCompatTests`).
- **`make device` works over Wi-Fi.** Detection now accepts `available (paired)`, not
  just `connected`.

## Privacy

- **New subsystems stay on-device.** Lexicons, swipe decoder, and learning store are
  local only. Apple Intelligence runs on-device and stays off by default. Nothing
  typed, completed, corrected, or learned leaves the device.
- **Translate stays on-device too.** Apple's Translation framework (or Apple
  Intelligence). Network is only Apple's one-time language-pack download. Custom
  dictionary is a local App Group file.
- Stopped tracking `.wrangler/cache/wrangler-account.json` and added `.wrangler/` to
  `.gitignore`.
