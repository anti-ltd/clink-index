Swipe typing, rebuilt. Swipe with two thumbs, mix taps and swipes in one word, and swipe through space for whole phrases. Japanese QWERTY and Pinyin work properly.

## New

- **Two-thumb swipe words, for real.** With two-thumb composition on, both thumbs can glide at the same time — m→n with one and e→a with the other is "mean" whichever thumb lifts first — and a letter tapped while the other thumb is still gliding joins that word (m→n with an e tap in the middle is "men"). No waiting, no mode to switch.
- **Any mix of taps and swipes makes a word.** Tap q, swipe u→i, tap t, space: "quit". Swipe w→a, tap i, tap t: "wait". Tap p, r, i, then swipe v→a and t→e: "private". A tap right after a swipe can finish the word it meant (swipe p→i→l, tap e: "pile"; tap l: "pill"), and a swipe that only gets part-way leaves its letters open for you to finish by tapping.
- **One swipe, several words.** Glide i→c→a→n→d→o→t→h→i→s without touching space and get "I can do this".
- **Swipe through space for whole phrases.** Glide h→e→y, keep going down into the space bar, come back up for t→h→e→r→e and lift: "hey there", each word committed the moment the finger crosses space and already counted as context for the next.

## Improved

- **Japanese QWERTY input works.** The Japanese (Romaji) layout now composes hiragana as you type and uses the existing Japanese dictionary for kanji conversion. Kana works without a downloaded pack, and saved Romaji selections survive older-settings migration.
- **Pinyin availability and presentation fixed.** App and keyboard builds now include the Chinese model, so missing, legacy, or corrupt downloaded packs cannot leave Pinyin unconverted. Candidates use Clink’s existing themed suggestion row; the segmented reading stays in the document and Space converts it.
- **Pinyin conversion rebuilt.** Simplified and Traditional Pinyin now share a word/bigram sentence decoder, with initial and mixed spellings, optional fuzzy Pinyin, prefix candidate commits, remembered phrase choices, contextual next words, and a scrollable candidate rail that expands into a paged grid. Space converts; Return keeps Latin input; Chinese punctuation follows a converted phrase. Downloaded Chinese packs now use the validated CIMP v2 binary format. Japanese kana tables retain their existing format.
- **Swipe decoding rebuilt.** Every finger is its own stroke with its timing; a beam search over the dictionary reads them together, so rounded corners, fast flicks, overshoot and slow dwells decode far more reliably (corner-cut top-1 roughly doubled on the synthetic held-out set) and a word commits in a few milliseconds instead of tens. The same frequency, personal-vocabulary, bigram and neural-context signals the tap corrector uses now rank swipe candidates exactly once, and a word swiped before the sentence context was ready is re-ranked when it arrives.

## Fixed

- **No swipe trail inside the space bar.** A glide that runs into space no longer paints a stub of trail on the bar; the stroke ends where it left the letters and starts again where it comes back.
