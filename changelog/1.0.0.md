First production release. A customizable, offline-first keyboard: container app plus
`ClinkKeyboard` extension sharing one `ClinkKit`, so the in-app preview is the real
keyboard view.

## New

- **Themes.** 30 built-in presets including six Liquid Glass variants (iOS 26;
  `.ultraThinMaterial` below). Match-system auto-switches light and dark.
- **Custom theme builder.** Solid or glass, font design, color pickers, gradient
  editor, photo backgrounds. Export/import `.clink` files.
- **Layouts & keys.** QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, Dvorak; optional number row and
  home-row inset; tunable height, width, spacing, roundness; balloon or glass
  popups; liquid press bloom.
- **Offline text assistance.** Autocomplete and autocorrect via `UITextChecker` and
  Damerau-Levenshtein ranking. Smart punctuation, auto-capitalize, return-to-letters.
- **Emoji keyboard.** Full Unicode 16.0 set, name search, skin tones, recents.
- **Clipboard history.** FIFO with pinning; inline bar or full overlay (needs Full
  Access).
- **Quick notepad.** Scratchpad or saved notes you can re-insert anywhere.
- **Panel switcher.** Open clipboard / notepad / emoji from the bar or 123 slide-up.
- **Sound & haptics.** Sound packs (system click works without Full Access); haptics
  need Full Access.
- **Spacebar cursor.** Slide, trackpad, or combined modes with tunable delay and
  sensitivity.
- **Container app.** Onboarding, enable flow, four-tab settings, live keyboard preview,
  `.clinkconfig` export/import.
- Device install pipeline (`make device`) and DEBUG-only AppStage capture routing.

## Privacy

- **Private by default.** Works without Full Access; no network on the typing path.
- All text assistance on-device via `UITextChecker`.
- **Full Access optional.** Only gates haptics and clipboard reads.
- Settings cross the app/extension boundary as App Group JSON via `SharedStore`.
- No analytics, crash reporters, accounts, or identifiers.
