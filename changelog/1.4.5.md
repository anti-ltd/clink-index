Stickers you can rotate, flip and snap to a grid. Completions stay on the suggestion bar.

## Improved

- **Sticker rotation & flips.** On Customize → Looks → Stickers, a rotation slider below size turns any placed sticker (the selection ring turns with it), a size glyph sits on the size slider, a rotation glyph on the rotation slider, and horizontal / vertical flip badges sit underneath.
- **Sticker grid snapping.** On the stickers page, a grid badge beside the flip badges snaps dragged and dropped stickers to a 20-division grid so they line up in rows and columns, with faint grid lines drawn over the stage while it's on.

## Fixed

- **Completions stay on the suggestion bar.** Learned words and ordinary dictionary completions no longer appear for a split second and then disappear when the settled typo pass arrives.
- **The swipe trail always shows now.** The stroke is painted by the same UIKit surface that captures the finger, removing the intermittent SwiftUI observation/compositing race entirely. A finished path also lingers and fades after lift, and long glides are downsampled so a busy device cannot starve the frames that show them.
